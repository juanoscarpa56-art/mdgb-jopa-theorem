Abstract

For centuries science has successfully explained planetary motion, financial markets and computational systems. Nevertheless, one fundamental question remained unanswered:

Can true love be demonstrated mathematically?

This work introduces the MDGB–JOPA Theorem, a mathematical framework proving that genuine love behaves simultaneously as an infinite constant, an optimal investment and a stable dynamic system whose solution converges toward permanent shared happiness.

1. Introduction

Since the earliest developments of modern mathematics, researchers have sought universal models capable of explaining complex natural phenomena.

Although economics successfully predicts markets and engineering accurately models computational systems, the dynamics of true love have traditionally remained outside the scope of formal scientific demonstration.

The present article proposes a rigorous mathematical formulation demonstrating that the interaction between M.D.G.B. and J.O.P.A. constitutes a globally stable system whose solution converges toward infinite happiness.

2. Fundamental MDGB–JOPA Theorem

Theorem

Let

\(M.D.G.B.\) = Mercí Dayana Guzmán Bustamante

\(J.O.P.A.\) = Juan Oscar Pacheco Andia

If both variables evolve under mutual trust, respect and unconditional affection, then the resulting emotional system satisfies

\[ \lim_{t\rightarrow\infty} \frac{M.D.G.B.+J.O.P.A.} {\text{Distance}} = \infty \]

Therefore, love remains bounded neither by time, nor distance, nor external perturbations.

3. Proof

Define the emotional state function

\[ L(t)=M.D.G.B.+J.O.P.A. \]

Differentiating with respect to time,

\[ \frac{dL}{dt}>0 \]

which indicates continuous emotional growth.

Likewise,

\[ \frac{dH}{dt}>0 \]

where \(H\) represents the global happiness of the system.

Since affection never decreases,

\[ \frac{d^{2}L}{dt^{2}}\ge0 \]

the system admits no unstable equilibrium.

Finally,

\[ \lim_{t\rightarrow\infty}L(t)=\infty \]
\[ Love(M.D.G.B.,J.O.P.A.)=\infty \]

Q.E.D.
